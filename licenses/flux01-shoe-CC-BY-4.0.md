# Flux 01 shoe model

The shoe TeraShoes ships, in `public/3d/flux01/`, is built by `pnpm assets:model` from the prototype's sample model:

- **Model:** "Materials Variants Shoe" © 2021 Shopify
- **Licence:** [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- **Source:** [Khronos glTF Sample Assets](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets)
- **Changes:** geometry Meshopt-compressed; the model's textures re-encoded as WebP; the colourway textures are the prototype's recolours of the original colour texture

**Product owner decision (21 Oct 2026, A1):** no new model; this is the shipping shoe. CC BY 4.0 allows commercial use, so the credit above must stay on the site. It is in the footer, as in the prototype.
